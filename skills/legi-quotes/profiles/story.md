# Profile: story

For books told as stories: spy missions (e.g. "Mossad"), wars, history told
as events, biographies. The owner loves tense, gripping stories, so a good
story is kept whole.

## Note forms

1. **Story card** - one whole story in one note. It may be long (300 words
   or more is fine). Never cut a good story to save space. Lines, each on its
   own line, no blank line between them:

   ```
   Bối cảnh: who, where, when, what was at stake.
   Diễn biến: what happened, in order, with the names and details that make it real.
   Khoảnh khắc nghẹt thở: the moment it could have failed.
   Kết cục: how it ended and what came after.
   Bài học: the method behind it, in one or two lines.
   ```

   Drop a line that has nothing to say. A very long story may be split into
   two or three cards (e.g. "Bắt Eichmann (1): tìm ra ông ta"), each still
   standing alone with names and the year.

2. **Nhân vật** - a short card on a person who comes back often: role, years,
   what they are known for. One per person, the first time they matter.
3. **Câu hay** - a memorable line someone said, with who, when and why.
4. **Nguyên tắc** - a method or rule of the trade the book shows or states
   (planning, cover stories, checking a source, patience, how a decision
   was made). Say which story it comes from.
5. **Bức tranh lớn** - at the end of a chapter or part, 1 to 3 cards on what
   the stories show together.

## What to pick

- every complete operation or episode with stakes,
- failures and mistakes as much as successes: they teach more,
- key decisions: who decided, with what information, what it cost,
- the people behind the work.

Skip: long setup with no point, praise with no fact behind it.

## Point of view

Story books often take one side. Write what happened as fact only when the
book gives it as fact. Put the book's praise or blame as "Theo sách, ...".
If the event is known to be disputed and you know it well, add one
`Ngoài sách:` line. Do not glorify killing; tell it plainly.

## Sections

One `#` per chapter or mission, named so it stands alone, with a year:
`# Bắt Eichmann (Argentina, 1960)`. Notes inside follow the story's order.
