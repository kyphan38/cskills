# Profile: practical

For skill books: speaking well, communication at work, negotiation, habits,
management. The goal is to use it tomorrow, so each note is something to do
or to say.

## Note forms

1. **Quy tắc** - "Khi <situation>, hãy <action>." Then one line on why it
   works. One technique per note.
2. **Câu mẫu** - words the user can say as they are, in Vietnamese, with the
   situation: "Khi sếp giao việc gấp: 'Em làm được, nhưng việc A sẽ chậm một
   ngày. Anh muốn ưu tiên việc nào?'"
3. **Sai → đúng** - a short pair that shows the change:
   "Sai: 'Anh sai rồi.' → Đúng: 'Em hiểu khác một chút, mình xem lại số này nhé?'"
4. **Câu hay** - a sharp line from the author, short.
5. **Bức tranh lớn** - at the end of a chapter, the 3 to 5 ideas that matter
   most, as a short list on separate lines.

## What to pick

- techniques with a clear situation and action,
- examples and stories only when they show a technique; keep them to one or
  two lines,
- warnings: what not to do and why.

Skip: motivation talk, long stories about the author, claims with no method.

## Style

Use "bạn" or the natural Vietnamese for the situation (anh, chị, em at work).
Make the sample words sound like real speech, not like a book.

## Sections

One `#` per chapter, named by the skill: `# Nói không mà không mất lòng`.
