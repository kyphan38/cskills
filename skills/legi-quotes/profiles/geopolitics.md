# Profile: geopolitics

For books on geography, politics, the history of countries, economics
(e.g. "Quyền lực của địa lý").

## Note forms

1. **Nguyên văn** - the author's sentence is clear and under ~40 words.
   Copy it (translate to Vietnamese if the book is English). Put the name in
   if it uses "it" or "this country"; then it becomes rút gọn.
2. **Rút gọn** - a good sentence with extra clauses cut away.
3. **Tóm tắt** - an idea spread over a passage, in 1 to 4 sentences.
4. **Bối cảnh** - a short card on a key person or term the reader needs, e.g.
   who Darius I was and how he was related to Cyrus II, or what split Sunni
   and Shia. Mostly outside knowledge: keep it short, well known and sure,
   with no `Ngoài sách` label.
5. **Bức tranh lớn** - when a chapter ends (or the user asks), 1 to 3 cards
   that link the chapter's ideas in a chain of causes:
   "Núi biến Iran thành pháo đài → nhưng chia cắt các dân tộc → nên chính
   quyền phải tập trung, cứng rắn → ..." This is what makes the notes a
   system, not a pile.

## What to pick

- the main claim of a section,
- clear cause and effect ("X vì Y", "X nên Y"),
- a surprising fact or number that supports a claim,
- a sharp sentence or a famous quote (e.g. Churchill on Persian oil),
- a turning point with its year.

Merge small facts on the same point into one note (e.g. the Kurds: number,
place, religion, uprising after 1979).

## Sections

One `#` per country or chapter if it is new, and `##` per topic inside it,
e.g. for Iran: `## Địa lý`, `## Dân tộc`, `## Dầu mỏ và Hormuz`,
`## Đế chế Ba Tư`, `## Hồi giáo và Shia`, `## Người Anh và dầu mỏ`.
Keep notes in time order inside a history section.
